NASA RAG Chat - Evaluation Dataset
===================================

This file lists the sample questions used to test the NASA Mission
Intelligence RAG system, grouped by category, along with the answer we
expected the system to produce (based on the source documents in
data_text/). These same questions are stored in machine-readable form in
test_questions.json and are consumed end-to-end by batch_evaluate.py,
which retrieves context, generates an answer, and scores it with RAGAS
(Response Relevancy, Faithfulness, and - since each question below has a
reference answer - BLEU, ROUGE, and Context Precision as well).

To reproduce these results yourself:
    python batch_evaluate.py --openai-key YOUR_KEY \
        --chroma-dir ./chroma_db_openai \
        --collection-name nasa_space_missions_text \
        --test-file test_questions.json \
        --output evaluation_results.json


1. OVERVIEW  (Apollo 11)
-------------------------
Q: What was the main objective of the Apollo 11 mission?
Expected answer: Apollo 11's primary objective was to land astronauts on
the Moon and return them safely to Earth, fulfilling President Kennedy's
goal of a crewed lunar landing before the end of the 1960s. The system
should cite the Apollo 11 flight plan / mission documents as its source.


2. EMERGENCY  (Apollo 13)
--------------------------
Q: What emergency occurred on Apollo 13 and when did it happen?
Expected answer: About 56 hours into the mission, an oxygen tank in the
service module ruptured after a short circuit, crippling power, water,
and life support and forcing the crew to abort the lunar landing. The
system should reference the Apollo 13 PAO/technical transcripts.


3. DISASTER ANALYSIS  (Challenger)
------------------------------------
Q: What caused the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster?
Expected answer: An O-ring seal in the right solid rocket booster failed,
allowing hot pressurized gas to escape; this, combined with unusually
cold launch-morning temperatures, led to the structural breakup of the
shuttle 73 seconds after liftoff. The system should draw on the
Challenger mission audio transcripts.


4. CREW  (Apollo 13)
----------------------
Q: Who were the astronauts on the Apollo 13 crew and how did they
   survive the accident?
Expected answer: The crew was Jim Lovell, Jack Swigert, and Fred Haise.
After the explosion they moved into the Lunar Module and used it as a
"lifeboat," rationing power, water, and oxygen for the return trip.


5. TECHNICAL  (Apollo 11)
----------------------------
Q: What technical issues did the Apollo 11 Lunar Module encounter during
   descent to the Moon?
Expected answer: The guidance computer triggered 1202 and 1201 program
alarms from a data overload, and the crew had to manually fly past a
boulder field to find a safe landing site.


6. TIMELINE  (Challenger)
----------------------------
Q: What is the timeline of events on the morning of the Challenger
   launch?
Expected answer: On January 28, 1986, the launch was delayed several
times due to cold overnight temperatures and ice on the pad, before
finally lifting off at 11:38 a.m. EST and breaking apart about 73
seconds later.


7. TECHNICAL  (Apollo 13)
----------------------------
Q: How did the Apollo 13 crew remove excess carbon dioxide from the
   Lunar Module cabin?
Expected answer: The crew and mission control improvised the "mailbox"
adapter from spacecraft materials (plastic bags, cardboard, tape) to fit
the Command Module's square lithium hydroxide canisters into the Lunar
Module's round receptacles.


Grading notes
-------------
- Every answer above should be grounded in the retrieved context (high
  Faithfulness) and directly address the question asked (high Response
  Relevancy).
- Because each question has a written reference answer, BLEU/ROUGE/
  Context Precision are also computed for these seven questions - these
  are lower-value "nice to have" metrics compared to Faithfulness and
  Response Relevancy, since a good answer can be worded very differently
  from the reference and still be fully correct.
- If the system cannot find relevant context for a question, it should
  say so explicitly rather than guessing (see the system prompt in
  llm_client.py).
